## 1. Master Canonical NAP Block

To establish dominant local pack authority in Google and Bing, the following Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) data must be copied **identically** without variation, abbreviation, or stylistic substitution across all external directory submissions:

```text
Business Name:     Jimmys Removals and Logistics LTD
Trading Name:      Jimmys Removals
Street Address:    100 Wharfside Street
Locality:          Birmingham
County / Region:   West Midlands
Postal Code:       B1 1RF
Country:           United Kingdom (GB)
Telephone:         0121 000 0000
International Tel: +44 121 000 0000
Primary Email:     info@jimmysremovalsltd.co.uk
Website URL:       https://www.jimmysremovalsltd.co.uk/
Company Reg No:    12345678 (England & Wales)
```

### Golden Consistency Rules:
1. **Never abbreviate:** Do not use "St" instead of "Street" or "Bham" instead of "Birmingham" on some directories and full spelling on others.
2. **Never keyword-stuff the Business Name:** Use `Jimmys Removals and Logistics LTD` (or trading name `Jimmys Removals` where character limits strictly mandate).
3. **Use the canonical WWW HTTPS URL:** Always submit `https://www.jimmysremovalsltd.co.uk/` with the trailing slash.
4. **Primary telephone:** Never use a disposable call tracking number as the visible listing number on directories; always use the primary local geographic Birmingham exchange `0121 000 0000`.

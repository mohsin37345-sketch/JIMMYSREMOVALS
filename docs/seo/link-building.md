## 6. Prohibited Tactics (Strict Exclusions)

To protect Jimmys Removals from Google algorithmic penalties and manual actions, the following tactics are strictly barred:
* **Private Blog Networks (PBNs):** Never purchase links from expired domains or blog networks.
* **Paid Link Insertion Networks / Fiverr Gigs:** Never purchase bulk links from automated link vendors.
* **Automated Directory Blasting:** Never submit to hundreds of low-quality generic web directories.
* **Keyword-Anchored Spam:** Avoid unnatural exact-match anchor text schemes (e.g. 50 links with "cheap removals Birmingham"). All backlinks must use branded, topical, or natural URL anchors (e.g. "Jimmys Removals", "jimmysremovalsltd.co.uk", "this moving guide").

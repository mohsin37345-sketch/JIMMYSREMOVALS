## Implementation & Internal Linking Rules
1. **Rule of 3:** Every published article must contain prominent, contextual in-content links to **two services** and **one location** page.
2. **Anchor text diversity:** Avoid raw URLs or "click here". Use descriptive topical anchors (e.g. "our [Birmingham house removals service](/removals/house-removals/birmingham/)").
3. **Conversion Callout:** Every informational article concludes with an inline quote generator or link to `/moving-cost-calculator/` to funnel organic searchers into the quote funnel.
